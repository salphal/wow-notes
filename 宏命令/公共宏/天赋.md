



## 切换天赋

```text


/run C_SpecializationInfo.SetActiveSpecGroup(C_SpecializationInfo.GetActiveSpecGroup() % 2 + 1)


/run C_SpecializationInfo.SetActiveSpecGroup(1)

/run C_SpecializationInfo.SetActiveSpecGroup(2)


```
